# GouriMapCam (Free GPS Camera)

**GouriMapCam** is a 100% privacy-centric, zero-telemetry, standalone open-source Android camera application designed to capture photos and videos stamped with authentic real-time coordinates, micro-location addresses, and accurate timestamps.

---

### 🎯 Key Objectives

* **Field & Academic Verification:** Provide authentic, geotagged proof for school/college projects, site inspections, fieldwork, infrastructure monitoring, and official reporting.
* **100% Privacy & Standalone Operation:** Zero advertisements, third-party trackers, or external analytical dependencies. User data never leaves the device, and the app operates completely standalone without interacting with other installed applications.
* **True Real-World Orientation:** Front-camera captures preserve the natural orientation of text, signboards, and documents without mirroring, accompanied by real-time coordinates, location details, and timestamps.

---

### 🌟 Key Features

* **Authentic Geotag Watermark:** Automatically overlays location name, GPS coordinates, date, and time onto captured photos.
* **Customizable Stamp Density:** Tap the live HUD badge to toggle between **Full Address** (Street, Sub-locality, Town, State, Coordinates) and **Minimal** (Coordinates only).
* **Un-mirrored Selfies:** Front camera captures save in natural orientation so documents, book covers, and background text remain completely readable.
* **Tap-to-Focus & Exposure Lock:**
  * **Quick Tap:** Meters focus and exposure with an animated amber ring (auto-cancels after 2 seconds).
  * **Long Press (1.5s):** Locks focus and exposure firmly, displayed with a minimal `🔒 Locked` badge. Tap elsewhere to release.
* **Full In-App Camera Controls:**
  * **Pinch-to-Zoom:** Smooth digital zoom with a live magnification pill.
  * **Torch Toggle:** Instant flashlight control for low-light rear-camera captures.
  * **Self-Timer:** Cycle between Off, 3s, 5s, and 10s countdowns with a prominent visual timer.
  * **Quick Camera Flip:** Dedicated toggle button and horizontal swipe gestures to switch between front and rear cameras.
* **Instant Gallery Shortcut:** In-app thumbnail preview of the most recent capture with direct access to your system gallery viewer.
* **Video Recording:** High-definition video recording with live recording timer and blinking duration indicator.

### 📥 Download & Installation

1. Go to the [Releases](https://github.com/shajimonpk/GouriMapCamera/releases) section of this repository.
2. Download the latest `GouriMapCam-v1.0.0.apk`.
3. Open the downloaded file on your Android device and tap **Install** (Enable *Allow from this source* in Android Settings if prompted).
4. Launch the app and grant Camera, Location, and Audio permissions to start capturing geotagged media.

---

### 🛡️ Privacy & Security Commitment

* **Zero Telemetry:** No user analytics, unique identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage metrics are collected.
* **On-Device Geocoding:** Location coordinates and street addresses are resolved strictly on-device using native Android system APIs without remote server tracking.
* **Scoped Media Storage:** Photos and videos are saved directly into standard public media directories (`DCIM/GouriMapCamera` and `Movies/GouriMapCamera`).
* **Standalone Sandboxing:** The app functions in complete isolation from other apps installed on your device.
